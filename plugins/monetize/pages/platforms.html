Testing ads before release

Real ads usually show only on the platform or in its test tool, not in a local build. Most platforms have one: Poki Inspector, the CrazyGames QA tool, the YouTube Playables Test Suite, the Playgama QA tool. Check there that the game pauses, mutes, continues after a missing ad and rewards only on success.

Principles checked 2026-11-26. Limits, revenue shares, SDK versions and commands change, so they are not copied here: every platform fact links to its owner's page. Read that page before you act on it.
