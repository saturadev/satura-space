# Code of Conduct

Satura Space is built in the open, and everyone who files an issue, reviews code or sends a pull
request is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or the engine they came from.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique the code and the idea, never the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful to the people who read the thread later.
- Respect a "no". If a maintainer or a contributor asks you to stop, stop.
- Credit other people's work and don't post their private information.

## What we don't accept

Harassment, insults, slurs, sexual content or attention, threats, trolling, and doxxing, in issues,
pull requests, discussions, commit messages or anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

Email [support@saturaspace.com](mailto:support@saturaspace.com) with links or screenshots. Reports
are read by the maintainers and kept private. Security vulnerabilities go through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and warn, block or ban anyone who breaks
these rules, in proportion to what happened. Decisions are explained to the people involved.
